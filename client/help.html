Help Center

Welcome to ⚡ ConnectHub Support! Below are answers to common questions about features, registration, short video clips, and moderation.

How do I register an account?

Go to Sign Up, fill in your profile details, confirm you are 13+, and enter your program invite code (e.g. CONNECT2026).

What is ⚡ ConnectClips?

ConnectClips is our TikTok-style vertical short video feed! Click Clips ⚡ in the top navigation bar to watch HD short videos with swipe navigation, sound controls, likes, and comment drawers.

How do I report inappropriate content or harassment?

Click the Report button on any post, clip, or user profile. Select a category (spam, harassment, hate speech, NSFW, etc.) and submit your report to moderation.

How do I block a user?

Visit the target user's profile page and click Block. Blocked users will no longer be able to message or view your activity.

Platform Security Tips

  • Keep your password unique and at least 8 characters long.
  • Never share invite passcodes or session tokens publicly.
  • Report safety concerns directly to moderators via the report system.
