Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026 · ⚡ ConnectHub Platform

⚡ ConnectHub is a full-stack social media and TikTok-style short video learning platform. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is secured, and how we protect user privacy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: Name, username, email address, password (hashed using bcrypt), and date of birth (13+ requirement).
  • User Created Content: Posts, ConnectClips short videos, comments, direct messages, stories, bookmarks, and uploaded media files.
  • Platform Analytics & Logs: Login timestamps, interactions, and security event logs.

2. How Information is Used

  • To deliver real-time social networking features, short video feeds, and messaging.
  • To investigate content reports, enforce community rules, and ensure platform safety.
  • To audit security and rate limits on API endpoints.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell personal data to third parties. Data is stored securely and accessible only to authorized platform moderators for safety and educational evaluation.

4. Age Requirements & Protection

Users must be at least 13 years of age. Accounts failing age verification during sign-up are automatically blocked from registration.

5. Data Access & Deletion

You may request account deletion or data exports at any time by contacting platform moderators or your program administrator.

6. Cookie Preferences & Local Storage

ConnectHub uses essential cookies and local storage for authentication sessions and theme preferences.
